What trail camera photo sorting software should do

A useful sorter should reduce repetitive review without hiding the original evidence. TrailEye keeps the source file connected to each result and lets you correct AI decisions. That matters because night images, partial animals, vegetation, weather and low-resolution captures can challenge any recognition model.

The goal is not to pretend that every prediction is perfect. The goal is to bring likely events forward, preserve context and make human review faster and more consistent.

Photos and videos in one workflow

Many trail-camera archives contain both still images and short clips. TrailEye handles photos and videos through the same folder-based workflow, so you do not need a separate manual process for each media type. Results can be reviewed in the application and connected to the wider archive.

Useful for more than wildlife

TrailEye’s first detection stage distinguishes animals, people and vehicles. This helps hunters and wildlife watchers focus on animal events, while land managers can also locate relevant human or vehicle activity. TrailEye does not identify individual people and should always be used in accordance with local privacy and camera-placement laws.

Before you start

Processing speed depends on file size, media type and Windows hardware. Compatible graphics hardware can accelerate local inference through DirectML. AI confidence also varies with distance, lighting, motion blur, camera placement and how much of the animal is visible. Important classifications should be checked by a person.
